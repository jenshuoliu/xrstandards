XR standardization community

Standardization in Extended Reality

XRStand brings together researchers, industry and standards bodies to discuss interoperability, reproducibility and open science in XR. We run workshops, tutorials and panels at conferences such as IEEE ISMAR and IEEE VR. This site collects them all, together with papers, talks and recordings.

    About

    XRStand is an informal community, not an official body of any conference or standards organization. Our activities are organized through the standardization committees of the hosting conferences, so the organizers differ from event to event. See each event’s Committee link for who was involved.
